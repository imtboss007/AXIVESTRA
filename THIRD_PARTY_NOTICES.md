## Motion Primitives — Animated Number (adapted)

Source: https://github.com/ibelick/motion-primitives/blob/main/components/core/animated-number.tsx

The AXIVESTRA component at `src/components/MotionAnimatedNumber.tsx` is an adaptation of Motion Primitives' Animated Number. The upstream Motion spring was replaced with a small `requestAnimationFrame` count interpolation, and display formatting uses the Indian locale. Only record/action counts use this component; monetary and valuation figures continue to use the existing exact currency formatter.
